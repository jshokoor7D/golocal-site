Why GoLocal

All your boards in one workspace.

Designed for governments to get more from their boards, and for the people who serve to get more from their time.

GoLocal comes out of direct experience with local boards and close work with the clerks, commissioners, and city staff who run them. It brings onboarding, board materials, and communication into one place.

Boards and commissions do real work for their communities, on housing, planning, the environment, and more, yet they have always run on tools made for something else. GoLocal is made for them, so a new appointee can find their footing quickly, a liaison can run a meeting without chasing documents, and the knowledge a board builds stays with the board.

Common questions

What governments ask first.

What is GoLocal?
GoLocal is a board management and compliance workspace that brings onboarding, documents, and communication into one place. It replaces fragmented email chains and personal drives with a secure center designed specifically for how local government operates. The result is less administrative overhead for clerks and liaisons, a modern digital workspace for volunteers, and protected institutional knowledge for the jurisdiction.
What types of boards and committees does it support?
GoLocal manages any appointed advisory, regulatory, or oversight body across a jurisdiction, including planning commissions, housing authorities, zoning boards, and ethics committees. It works whether you are running a single board or overseeing dozens of committees across a city or county.
How does GoLocal handle Open Meetings Law and FOIA compliance?
Compliance is built into how GoLocal works. Because staff send announcements and members reply privately, no reply-all thread forms on the platform, and giving members a sanctioned channel reduces the pull toward the group texts and email chains that create accidental serial meetings. The result is an organized, auditable record of official communication that cuts the time it takes to search for and produce records under a FOIA request.
What makes the appointee onboarding module different?
GoLocal includes a customizable onboarding builder. Staff can build structured orientation paths using eight built-in content types, including documents, training videos, acknowledgments, and custom quizzes. That lets you handle city-wide ethics compliance alongside board-specific training, and track completion in real time so members arrive at their first meeting prepared.
What happens when a new board member joins?
When a member is appointed, the staff liaison invites them using their preferred email address, and GoLocal handles the welcome from there. It walks the new member through a short tour and opens your custom onboarding module, and the liaison or board chair can add a personal welcome message right in the app. The new member arrives ready to serve, without a chain of manual setup emails.
Do board members have to log in from a computer every time?
No. GoLocal is mobile first, so members are not tied to a desktop. Most board members are working professionals serving on evenings and weekends, so they can review meeting packets, track their tasks, and pull up the bylaws right from a phone or tablet. The board's business stays organized and separate from their personal email and files.
Who owns our data?
Your jurisdiction owns its data. The institutional knowledge a board builds stays with the jurisdiction even after members leave, and we will walk your team and your attorney through exactly how data is handled and how you can export it whenever you want.
How does implementation work for our jurisdiction?
Implementation takes no technical heavy lifting from your team. We handle the initial setup, importing your active rosters, boards, and historical reference documents into the platform. We train your staff liaisons and clerks directly, so your administration starts saving hours on meeting prep right away.
