Why GoLocal

All your boards in one workspace.

Designed for governments to get more from their boards, and for the people who serve to get more from their time.

GoLocal comes out of direct experience with local boards and close work with the clerks, commissioners, and city staff who run them. It brings onboarding, board materials, and communication into one place.

Boards and commissions do real work for their communities, on housing, planning, the environment, and more, yet they have always run on tools made for something else. GoLocal is made for them, so a new appointee can find their footing quickly, a clerk can run a meeting without chasing documents, and the knowledge a board builds stays with the board.

Common questions

What governments ask first.

What is GoLocal?
A single workspace for running local government boards and commissions. It brings onboarding, meetings, documents, and communication into one place, replacing the scatter of email, paper packets, and personal drives.
What kinds of boards is it for?
Any appointed board or commission, advisory, regulatory, or oversight, from planning and housing to parks, ethics, and beyond. It works for a single board or for many across a jurisdiction.
Do our members have to learn another system?
No. Members still receive email and can reply from it. Only the staff liaison composes inside the app. There is no new inbox for members to check and nothing for them to learn.
Can members use it on their phones?
Yes. GoLocal is built mobile first, so commissioners can read packets, mark tasks done, and keep up from a phone, which is how most volunteer service actually happens, in the evenings and between everything else.
How do we get started?
It begins with a short conversation about how your boards work today. If it is a fit, we handle the setup, bringing your board, roster, and existing documents in before anyone on your side touches it. The lift is ours, not yours. Your staff liaison works in the app, and your members keep using email the way they already do.
What does it cost?
Pricing depends on your jurisdiction and how many boards you run. The honest next step is a conversation, so any number reflects what you actually need rather than a guess.
